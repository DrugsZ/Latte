# Code of Conduct

Latte follows a simple rule: be respectful, be constructive and help keep the project useful for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback, especially in code review.
- Keep discussions focused on the project, its users and its maintainability.
- Assume good intent, but clarify when a decision affects architecture, safety or licensing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably judge harmful to the project community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments; close issues; reject pull requests; or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately. Include context, links and screenshots when possible.

## Attribution

This document is intentionally short and project-specific. If the community grows, Latte may adopt a fuller policy such as Contributor Covenant.
